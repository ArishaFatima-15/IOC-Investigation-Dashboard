SPL Queries Used In Dashboard Panels:

1. SOC Main Triage Feed: 
index="ioc_investigation" | table timestamp ioc_value ioc_type severity status assigned_analyst incident_id malware_printable 
 
2. Severity Breakdown: 
index="ioc_investigation" | stats count by severity 
 
3. Incident Campaign Correlation: 
index="ioc_investigation" | stats values(ioc_value) as Associated_IOCs, values(ioc_type) as Types count by incident_id, malware_printable 
 
4. Threat Types by Severity: 
index="ioc_investigation" | chart count by ioc_type severity
 
5.Historical Trend View: 
index="ioc_investigation" | timechart span=1h count by severity
